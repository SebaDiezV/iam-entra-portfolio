## GDPR Compliance Mapping — Phase 1

| GDPR Article | Requirement | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| Art. 5(1)(d) | Accuracy | CSV validation rejects incomplete records |
| Art. 5(1)(f) | Confidentiality | Cryptographic password generation, SecureString |
| Art. 25 | Privacy by Design | Least-privilege group assignment by default |
| Art. 30 | Records of Processing | Timestamped audit log per onboarding session |
